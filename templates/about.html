About Sreeyogakshetra

WHO WE ARE

Nurturing Balance & Inner Well-Being

Sreeyogakshetra is a dedicated yoga and wellness center committed to promoting health, balance, and inner well-being through the timeless practice of yoga.

With over 15 years of experience, we offer expert guidance through both offline and online sessions, making yoga accessible to individuals of all ages and fitness levels. We believe that yoga is not just an exercise system, but a pathway to holistic structural stability and internal peace.

Meet Our Team

Founder & Director

Dr. Sreeja

Dr. Sreeja is the founder and director of Sreeyogakshetra, a leading yoga studio established in 2012. With extensive expertise in hatha yoga, vinyasa yoga, prenatal (pregnancy) yoga, and kids yoga, she has dedicated her career to promoting holistic health and well-being through authentic yogic practices. Her commitment to excellence and contribution to the field of yoga have been recognized with the prestigious Bharat Gaurav Ratna Award. Through Sreeyogakshetra, Dr. Sreeja has empowered thousands of individuals to achieve physical, mental, and emotional wellness, making the institution one of the most respected yoga centers in Salem.

Director & Instructor

Mr. Agastthya

Mr. Agastthya is the director of Sreeyogakshetra and a certified yoga instructor with over 18 years of dedicated yoga practice and more than 2 years of teaching experience. He specializes in hatha yoga, vinyasa yoga, pranayama, and kids yoga, helping individuals of all ages improve their physical, mental, and emotional well-being. In addition to his expertise in traditional yoga disciplines, Agastthya is also a certified aerial yoga instructor. As the director of Sreeyogakshetra, he is committed to promoting holistic wellness and delivering high-quality yoga education through personalized instruction, corporate wellness programs, and community outreach initiatives.
